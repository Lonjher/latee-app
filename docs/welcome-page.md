## Content sources

Read on 2026-09-20:
- https://pp.annuqayahlatee.id/ — identity, complexes, address, contact, social links.
- https://pp.annuqayahlatee.id/tentang/ — founding history and educational mission.
- https://pp.annuqayahlatee.id/program/pendidikan/ — Madrasah Diniyah, Quran and kitab studies, SKIA.
- https://pp.annuqayahlatee.id/latee-i-putri/ — program areas for Latee I.
- https://pp.annuqayahlatee.id/latee-ii-putri/ — program areas for Latee II.
- https://pp.annuqayahlatee.id/santri-baru/ — registration guidance per complex.

Copy is condensed and paraphrased to fit the existing layout. Enrollment counts and fees are not presented as current facts; the registration CTA links to the maintained official page. Content is a static snapshot, not an automated sync.

Images in `public/images/pesantren` come from the official site's public uploads. Captions describe the source documentation without asserting the photos depict a specific female complex. Registration and contact links open the official site; email opens the user's mail application.
